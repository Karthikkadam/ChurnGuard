    About ChurnGuard

    Understanding the model and its predictions

    🎯

    What is Customer Churn?

    Customer churn (attrition) is the loss of clients or customers. It is commonly expressed as the percentage of subscribers who discontinue their subscriptions within a given period.

    🤖

    How Does It Work?

    ChurnGuard uses an XGBoost classifier trained on 10,000 customer records. The model analyzes 10 features including credit score, age, geography, balance, and activity status to predict churn probability.

    📊

    Key Features

    The most influential factors are Age, Estimated Salary, and Credit Score. Customers from Germany and inactive members show higher churn rates.

    ⚡

    Model Performance

    XGBoost outperformed 8 other algorithms including Neural Networks, Gradient Boosting, and Random Forest, achieving the highest accuracy on test data.
